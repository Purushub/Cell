IB Cell Biology Field Dossier

1. Cell Basics

All living things are made of cells. Cells are microscopic units containing organelles suspended in cytoplasm.

2. Plant vs. Animal Cells

Plant: Cell wall, chloroplasts, large vacuole.
Animal: Flexible membrane, lysosomes, small temporary vacuoles, centrioles.

3. Specialised Cells

Red blood cells (biconcave, no nucleus), Neurons (long axon, dendrites), Root hair cells (huge surface area).

4. Hierarchy

Cell → Tissue → Organ → Organ System → Organism.
